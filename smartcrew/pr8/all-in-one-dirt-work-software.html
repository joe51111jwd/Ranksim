All-in-one dirt work software

All-in-one dirt work software

Andrew Drish, founder
Published October 29, 2026. Updated October 29, 2026

SmartCrew is all-in-one dirt work software for dirtwork, excavation and sitework contractors with 5 to 100 employees. Dirtwork, one word or two, is the same work: your crews, your iron and your loads after the bid. AGTEK and STACK are takeoff tools. SmartCrew runs the job.

The short answer

All-in-one dirt work software, for a company our size, is the app the crew uses and the screen that shows job profit. Takeoff is a different tool, and you should keep it.

Use AGTEK, InSite or HeavyBid to bid the dirt. Use SmartCrew for clock-ins, machines, haul tickets, daily reports and the daily profit.

  • One phone app: GPS clock-in, the machine, inspections, haul tickets and the daily report.
  • One office screen: timecards, the shop queue, hauling and job profit against the bid.
  • It does not do earthwork takeoff, cut and fill, or machine control.
  • Built only for dirtwork and heavy civil companies with 5 to 100 employees.

What all-in-one means for dirt work

Dirt work software shows up as three different products. Takeoff measures the plans. Machine control drives the iron. Running the job is the hours, the loads and the profit. All-in-one, in SmartCrew, means that third job is one app, not a stack of time clocks, spreadsheets and paper tickets.

Dirtwork and dirt work are the same contractors

People spell it both ways. Dirtwork is one word. Dirt work is two. Excavation and sitework are the trades inside it. SmartCrew is built for that work only, for companies with 5 to 100 employees. It is not general contracting software, and it is not a takeoff program.

Who it is for

  • Pick SmartCrew if you have 5 to 100 people, you run your own crews and iron, and you want time, machines, loads and job profit in one app.
  • Pick AGTEK if the job is earthwork takeoff. Gradework is their sitework takeoff, and the same model can go to GPS machine control.
  • Pick STACK if you want cloud takeoff and estimating for sitework and earthwork: cut and fill, trench volumes and proposals. Their page also lists integrations with Acumatica, Procore and Sage.
  • Pick HCSS if you want earthwork estimating in HeavyBid and a field and fleet suite beside it. See HCSS alternative.

Most dirtwork companies keep both halves. A takeoff tool to win the bid. SmartCrew after it. We do not replace AGTEK, and AGTEK's takeoff page does not replace the crew's clock-in.

Questions people ask

What is all-in-one dirt work software?

For a dirtwork contractor, it is one app for crew time, machines, haul tickets, daily reports and job profit. It is not the same thing as earthwork takeoff software.

Is dirtwork software the same as dirt work software?

Yes. Dirtwork and dirt work are two spellings of the same work. SmartCrew uses both, and it is built for excavation and sitework contractors with 5 to 100 employees.

Does SmartCrew do earthwork takeoff?

No. SmartCrew does not do takeoff, cut and fill or machine control. Keep AGTEK, InSite, HeavyBid or the estimating tool you already use, and enter the bid budget on the job.

How is SmartCrew different from AGTEK and STACK?

AGTEK Gradework and STACK's sitework product are takeoff and estimating tools. SmartCrew starts after the bid: the crew's hours, the machines, the loads and the daily profit.

Is SmartCrew for excavation and sitework contractors?

Yes. Excavation, grading, sitework, utilities, septic, land clearing and hauling all sit in the same band: dirtwork and heavy civil companies with 5 to 100 employees.

What does SmartCrew not do?

Estimating, takeoff, machine control, telematics hardware, hours-of-service logs and payroll processing. It does connect to QuickBooks Online, and it does send payroll-ready timecards.
