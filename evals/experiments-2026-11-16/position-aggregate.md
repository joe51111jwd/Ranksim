No method beats persistence on exact position. The best Nov 16 exact rate is ridge content with Bradley-Terry or Borda, 95/299 (0.318), against persistence 106/299 (0.355). The best Nov 13 exact rate is 90/298 (0.302), against persistence 100/298 (0.336).

Several ensemble ranges beat persistence on coverage, by a few rows. The best is the ridge off-page bootstrap ensemble, 194/299 (0.649) on Nov 16 against persistence 180/299 (0.602), and the ridge-plus-LambdaMART seed ensemble, 189/298 (0.634) on Nov 13 against 187/298 (0.628). Copeland, Bradley-Terry, Borda, and median rank barely change the order once the same ensemble votes are in hand. None of the 26 methods reach 85% exact or 95% coverage. The oracle fixed offset on the eval window is also about 0.57 to 0.63, so a better centering of a 3-wide window does not close the gap.
