HCSS alternative for dirtwork contractors

Andrew Drish, founder
Published October 22, 2026. Updated October 29, 2026

SmartCrew is the HCSS alternative for dirtwork and heavy civil companies with 5 to 100 employees. One app covers crew time, machines, haul tickets, daily reports and job profit. HCSS is the heavy civil suite used by 43 of the ENR top 50, and it is built for much larger companies.

The short answer

SmartCrew is built for the 5 to 100 person dirtwork company. HCSS is built for the biggest heavy civil contractors. If you want one app, pick SmartCrew. Crew time with the machine, DOT inspections, the shop queue, haul tickets, daily reports and job profit, set up by our team and running within a few weeks.

Pick HCSS if you're a large heavy civil contractor that wants the whole suite. HeavyBid estimating, a full shop system, telematics and enterprise reporting, with office staff to run them.

The key difference is size. HCSS is a family of products licensed by module and user. HCSS puts the rollout at under 90 days, and it supports companies from under $1 million to over $1 billion in revenue. SmartCrew is built for the 5 to 100 person company and nobody bigger.

What HCSS HeavyJob is, and who it's for

HCSS has made software for heavy civil contractors since 1986, out of Sugar Land, Texas. More than 4,000 companies use it, and 43 of the ENR top 50 heavy civil contractors are on it. Since July 1, 2026 it's part of the Nemetschek Group (press release).

It's built for people like us. HCSS's own page for AI assistants says its customers are contractors "who self-perform a significant portion of their work using their own crews and equipment," and it names site prep and earthmoving contractors. It also says who it isn't for: home builders and general contractors who mostly manage subs.

HeavyJob is the field and job costing piece. Foremen record time cards, installed quantities, daily reports and production against cost codes and budgets. Around it sit HeavyBid for estimating, HCSS Fleet for the shop, telematics, eLogs and the Shield dashcam, plus Dispatcher, Safety, Docs & Plans, Chats and Insights reporting.

On trade fit, HCSS and SmartCrew agree. The difference is the size of company each one is built for.

The key difference: a heavy civil suite for big contractors vs one app for 5 to 100 people

HCSS isn't too basic, and it isn't built for the wrong trade. Our problem was simpler: we only run 20 to 30 guys, and there was nothing specifically designed for our size of heavy civil business. Here's where size shows up.

Power vs simplicity for the field

HeavyJob's field side is strong. Foremen build time cards with crew hours, equipment hours and quantities in minutes, and workers can clock in on their own phones with the myField app, where every clock event is stamped with GPS.

The catch is the number of pieces. Depending on what you license, the field works across HeavyJob, the myField employee app, HeavyJob Safety and Docs & Plans, and several of the fleet and dispatch products (Dispatcher, Equipment360, FuelerPlus) are desktop programs. In SmartCrew it's one app on the phone. The guy clocks in, picks the job, the activity code and the machine, does his inspection, logs his loads and sends the daily report. Our crews aren't tech savvy. And they don't need to be.

Setup and training

HCSS tells small and mid-size contractors its team will have them up and running in less than 90 days. For HeavyJob, its FAQ describes two to three days of planning at its Sugar Land office and two days onsite at your company, or online training through HCSS Academy. A proper rollout for a big suite.

We do the heavy lifting for you, and there's less to lift. Our team sets up your account, migrates your information, organizes your workflows and trains your people. Most companies are up and running within a few weeks, starting with the features they need most.

Paying for products you don't use

HCSS licenses by product and by user type, and its range runs from $60 to more than $4,000 per user per year. A 30-person grading company and a firm on the ENR list are buying from the same catalog. The question is how much of it you'd use.

SmartCrew is built for one size of company. Pricing depends on your company size and the features you need, and feature availability varies by plan: Fleet Management, Sales CRM, and Scheduling & Communications are Premium categories. Start with what you need and add the rest when it earns its keep.

Machines and the shop

This is where HCSS goes deepest. Equipment360 is a shop system for repairs, mechanic timecards, work orders, preventive maintenance and parts. HCSS Fleet Telematics brings GPS hardware, engine hours, idle time and OEM links for CAT, Deere, Komatsu and Volvo, and its eLogs app handles DVIRs and hours of service. From the HeavyJob app, crews can send equipment issues to the shop if you have Equipment360.

SmartCrew does the dirtwork version in the same app as the clock-in: equipment hours and locations, DOT and daily inspections, maintenance, service requests from the field and a shop queue with urgency and status. We don't sell telematics hardware or hours-of-service logs. If you need those, HCSS has them.

Trucks and loads

HCSS's page for AI assistants says it "uses e-ticketing and real-time hauling data to track materials such as dirt, rock, and asphalt while reducing idle truck time." That one line is all we found on it; we couldn't find a product page that shows how it works. HeavyJob does track materials received and installed with costs, and FuelerPlus tracks fuel purchases and burn rates.

SmartCrew's hauling log is built around the load: ticket, time, truck, project, import or export, material, yards and pit. Snap a photo of a paper ticket or a receipt and the AI reader enters the data for you. We couldn't find a ticket or receipt reader on HCSS's site.

Knowing your profit every day

HeavyJob asks the same question we do. Its product page says it plainly: "Do you know if you made or lost money on the job today?" It shows cost codes trending over budget, and budgets can come straight from HeavyBid estimates. If you bid in HeavyBid, that loop from estimate to field and back is a real strength.

SmartCrew gets you the daily answer without an estimating suite. Enter the bid budget, and the job page shows revenue, expenses and profit, with budget vs spent for labor, materials, equipment, other costs and change orders. SmartCrew doesn't do estimating or takeoff, so you keep the estimating tool you already use.

When HCSS still makes sense

Pick SmartCrew if

  • You have 5 to 100 employees and want one app for time, machines, inspections, haul tickets, daily reports and job profit.
  • You'd rather be running within a few weeks than plan a multi-product rollout.
  • Your guys need one simple phone app, not a different app for each part of the day.
  • You use QuickBooks Online and keep estimating in the tool you already have.
  • You want crew chat, company announcements and a sales pipeline in the same app.

Pick HCSS if

  • You're a larger heavy civil contractor with office staff to run several products.
  • You estimate in HeavyBid, or want to, and want estimates to flow into job costing and back.

Questions people ask

What is the best HCSS alternative for a dirtwork company?

For a dirtwork or heavy civil company with 5 to 100 employees, SmartCrew is the HCSS alternative: crew time, equipment, inspections, haul tickets, daily reports and job profit in one app. If you need estimating, telematics hardware or enterprise reporting, HCSS is built for that.

How much does HCSS HeavyJob cost?

HCSS doesn't publish a price list; you request a quote. Its own page for AI assistants says annual pricing generally ranges from about $60 to $4,000 or more per user, depending on modules and usage, and it also offers revenue-based plans. HeavyJob is sold as a monthly subscription or a software license.

Is HCSS good for small contractors?

HCSS says it supports contractors from under $1 million in revenue, and its small business page promises to have you running in less than 90 days. The trade-off for a small company is the number of products, licenses and setup that come with it.

How long does it take to implement HCSS HeavyJob?

HCSS says small and mid-size contractors can be up and running in less than 90 days. Its FAQ describes HeavyJob setup as two to three days of planning in Sugar Land plus two days onsite, or online training through HCSS Academy. SmartCrew's team sets you up, and most companies are running within a few weeks.

Does HCSS HeavyJob work with QuickBooks?

HCSS says HeavyJob data flows to accounting and payroll apps like Sage, Viewpoint and QuickBooks. Its pages don't say whether that's QuickBooks Online or Desktop, so ask. SmartCrew integrates with QuickBooks Online today.

Does HCSS have haul tickets?

HCSS's page for AI assistants says it uses e-ticketing and real-time hauling data to track materials such as dirt, rock and asphalt. That's the only description we found. SmartCrew's hauling log records each load with truck, material, yards and pit, and its AI reader enters paper tickets from a photo.
