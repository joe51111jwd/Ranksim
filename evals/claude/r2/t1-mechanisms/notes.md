# Round 2, Target 1 research: where a URL new to a search's top 10 lands, and why

Design data only. SERP rows through 2026-11-17, loaded only through `scripts.claude.rsdata` (via `t1_common.design_rows` and `t1_common.emulation_rows`). The script asserts that the maximum loaded day is 2026-11-17. No Serper call, no network, no direct SQLite read, no file outside this folder written. This is a measurement of mechanisms, not a predictor to freeze. Any derived position used as a guess is PREDICTED.

Reproduce: `python scripts/claude/r2_t1_mechanisms.py` (about 10 seconds). It writes `results.md` (every table, generated by the script) and `results.json` (every number plus one record per entrant) in this folder. Every number below is copied from `results.md`.

Conventions:

- Slices are the round 1 design slices. "Pooled" means the six real slices (o30 Nov 9, 11, 13, 16, 17 and rev Nov 17). The a70 emulation reuses the Nov 17 o30 outcome with the history cut to the Nov 16 q100 pull plus the Nov 16 deep list, so it is always shown on its own and never pooled.
- Check: the script reproduces round 1. The frozen rule gets band3 148/303 on pooled new rows.
- c9 and c7 are constants. "dp" is the domain prior (the site's best consensus position on this search on the previous pulled day, 11 if none). "frozen" is the round 1 rule called read-only through `t1_placement.predict_rows`.
- Every derived position is rounded and clipped to [2, 9], like the frozen rule. With truth in 1 to 10 that clip never lowers band3 or err3.
- Intervals: query bootstrap, 2000 draws, seed 20261119 (`metrics.py`).

## Bottom line

Supported by the data:

1. Newcomers land low because that is where the slots open. Leave rate rises from 23/163 at position 1 to 82/142 at position 10. Landing is tied to the vacated slots beyond what the landing distribution alone explains, but only a little: pooled new rows land within 1 of a vacated slot 256 times out of 301 against a permutation null of 231.1 [220, 242].
2. When the entrant lands above the vacated slot, it is an insertion. The survivors in between move down by exactly one 36/61 times, against 14/150 for the other survivors.
3. Same-site URL swaps carry the site anchor. 84 of 303 new rows are a swap (a URL of the same site left that day), only 17 are an addition. On swaps the swapped slot gets band3 40/84 against 24/84 for c9. The swap effect is strongest for reddit (13/19 against 3/19).
4. Two signals flag top-3 entrants on every one of the 7 slices: the site already holds a top-3 slot on this search (new rows: 20/48 land top 3 against 35/255) and the URL already ranks top 3 on a sibling search (16/40 against 39/263).
5. Reentrants land where they last showed. The last pull that showed the URL is the best anchor (pooled band3 79/127), and a flicker the day before is the tightest of all (o30 Nov 17 only: 16/27 exact, mean abs error 0.78).

Rejected by the data (for placement of new rows):

- Sibling-search transfer of the URL's position as a point estimate, in every form tried (best sibling, max-overlap sibling, overlap-weighted, relative placement among shared URLs). On pooled new rows none beats c9 or the frozen rule on band3, and none beats c7 on err3. Weighting by list overlap does not make the transfer better.
- Site-level sibling transfer (site ranks elsewhere, URL does not): worse than c9 and the frozen rule.
- Sibling evidence where the frozen rule falls back to its default 9: 33 or 34 of 73 against 39 for the default.
- The "first unstable slot" of yesterday's pulls as a slot: it is position 1 for 27 of 45 rows, so it carries no slot information.

Cannot be tested on design data:

- Brand in the search: 8 entrants carry it (2 new). The a70 searches have many brand searches, but no a70 outcome exists before Nov 18.
- A three-day gap with a deep list in history, which is exactly the a70 Nov 19 setup. Every design slice with a deep list has a one-day gap.
- Siblings fresher than the target (o30 pulled Nov 18 and 19 morning, a70 last pulled Nov 16). On every design slice the sibling evidence comes from the same previous day as the target's own history.
- Title match, as a meaningful test: only 140 of 303 new rows have a stored title from history, and 14 of them match.

## Q2. Sibling-search transfer

Evidence is the URL (or its site) in another tracked search's consensus top 10 on that search's latest history day. The "previous pulled day" window and the "last 3 days" window give identical sibling sets on every design slice (0 rows differ), because every sibling's latest day equals the target's previous day. J is the Jaccard overlap of URL keys between the target's previous-day top 10 and the sibling's top 10. "Relative" places the URL among the URLs both searches share, by its order in the max-overlap sibling: mid = halfway between the target positions of the nearest shared URLs above and below it, insert = one below the shared URL above it.

## What this suggests for the theory (not tested here)

- Model the page as slots, not as scores. Entrants mostly fill slots vacated at the bottom, by insertion. A predictor of who leaves (bottom position, missing from a pull yesterday) is the natural next step for the o30 days with multi-pull history. It cannot help a70 on Nov 19.
- The site anchor is a swap detector. Its value depends on swaps being durable, and on the one three-pull, three-day-gap day (o30 Nov 16) it failed. Expect the frozen rule's edge over 9 on a70 Nov 19 to rest mostly on the deep list, which has never been seen at a three-day gap.
- Sibling transfer should be used, if at all, as a top-3 flag, not as a position.
