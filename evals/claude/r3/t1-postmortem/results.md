## 6. Candidate signals (each rule fixed in the script before it was scored)

Rule form: a flag rule predicts 4 for a flagged row and 7 otherwise (U3 predicts 9 when flagged). A point rule predicts the signal clipped to 2 to 9, else 7. 'Cross-fit' replaces the fixed values: each search group's rows get, for their stratum, the band3-best constant chosen on the other search groups (leave one search group out). A point rule's cross-fit uses three strata: signal 1 to 5, signal 6 or worse, no signal. 'Fitted constant' is the same cross-fit with one stratum, so 'cross-fit minus fitted constant' is what the signal adds beyond picking the pool's best constant. Halves A and B are a seeded split of the search groups. 'Survived' = the fixed rule beats flat 7 overall and in both halves, or the cross-fit beats flat 7 and beats the fitted constant overall and in both halves (fitted on one half, scored on the other). The comparison with the fitted constant was added after the first run, because on the reentrant pool every cross-fit 'beat' flat 7 only by picking 9.
