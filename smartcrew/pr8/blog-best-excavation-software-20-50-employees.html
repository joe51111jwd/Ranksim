Best all-in-one dirt work and excavation software for a team of 20 to 50

Andrew Drish, founder
Published October 28, 2026. Updated October 29, 2026

For an excavation or dirtwork company with 20 to 50 people, the best all-in-one software is SmartCrew. That's the size we built it for. Our own family company runs 20 to 30 guys. One app covers timecards, machines, haul tickets, daily reports, safety and job profit. Your crew uses it on their phones. You see every job against the bid.

The short answer

A team of 35 is too big for a time clock app and too small for enterprise software. That's the gap SmartCrew fills.

At 20 to 50 people you have several crews on several jobs, a shop, trucks and one or two people in the office. The apps built for 1 to 4 people run out. The suites built for 500 people need a full-time admin.

If your work is mostly estimates and homeowner invoices, Projul or JobTread may fit better. If you run your own iron and trucks, it's SmartCrew.

The short version

  • Best all-in-one pick for a dirtwork or excavation company with 20 to 50 people: SmartCrew.
  • Best if you only need a time clock with equipment hours: BusyBusy.
  • Best if you mostly estimate and invoice homeowners: Projul or JobTread.
  • Best if you're past 100 people and want a full heavy civil suite: HCSS.
  • Most teams of 35 run two tools: takeoff software like InSite or AGTEK for cut and fill, and SmartCrew as the operations hub for everything after the bid.
  • At 35 people, seat caps decide the price. Some plans stop at 10 or 20 users.
  • SmartCrew pricing depends on your size and features. You get a number on a short call with Andrew.

Do you need a two-part stack? Takeoff software plus an operations hub

Yes, for most excavation teams of 20 to 50. Use a takeoff tool like InSite or AGTEK for cut and fill, and SmartCrew as the operations hub for everything after the bid. The takeoff tool does the earthwork math. SmartCrew runs the crews, the iron and the trucks. The full pages are all-in-one dirt work software and all-in-one heavy civil software.

Most construction apps are good at the business side: scheduling, crew time, invoicing. They don't do the heavy engineering side: cut and fill takeoffs, 3D grading surfaces. Takeoff software does that math. It doesn't run your week. So you need both, and each one should do its own job well.

What changes when an excavation company hits 20 people?

The owner stops seeing every job. That's it. At 8 guys you can drive past every site before lunch. At 35 you can't.

  • Timecards come from four foremen, not one. Each one fills them out his own way.
  • Machines move between jobs. Nobody's sure which job the dozer hours belong to.
  • Tickets pile up. Loads come in from several trucks and pits, on paper, in the dash.
  • The shop gets busy. Repairs come in by phone call and text, and some get lost.
  • The office is still one or two people. They type it all in at night.

So a 35 person company needs the field to put the data in once, on the phone, at the job. And it needs the office to see it the same day. You don't need more software. You need fewer places to look.
