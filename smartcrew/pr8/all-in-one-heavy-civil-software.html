All-in-one heavy civil software

All-in-one heavy civil construction software

Andrew Drish, founder
Published October 29, 2026. Updated October 29, 2026

SmartCrew is all-in-one heavy civil construction software for dirtwork and heavy civil companies with 5 to 100 employees. One app covers crew time, machines, haul tickets, daily reports and job profit. HCSS, Trimble, Sage and B2W sell larger suites that also include estimating, accounting or payroll, which SmartCrew does not.

The short answer

All-in-one, for a 5 to 100 person heavy civil company, means the field and the job cost live in one app. It does not mean estimating, machine control and a full accounting system.

If you want HeavyBid, payroll and an ERP from the same vendor, HCSS, Trimble or Sage is built for that. If you run your own crews and iron and want them in one phone app, that is SmartCrew.

  • HCSS, Trimble Construction One, Sage and B2W are the suites built around estimating, accounting or a bigger rollout.

What all-in-one means for heavy civil

On a heavy civil job the money is in hours. Crew hours, machine hours and truck hours, against the bid. All-in-one means those hours, the loads and the daily report land in one place, and the office sees profit the same day.

These are the modules SmartCrew actually has. Scheduling, sales and crew communication are Premium categories.

Who it is for

SmartCrew is for heavy civil and dirtwork companies with 5 to 100 employees that self-perform with their own crews and iron. Roads, utilities, sitework, grading, excavation. Andrew's family company runs 20 to 30 people, and that is the size it was built for.

  • Pick SmartCrew if you want one phone app for time, machines, inspections, haul tickets, daily reports and job profit, and you already have an estimating tool.
  • Pick HCSS if you are a large heavy civil contractor and want HeavyBid, HeavyJob and the fleet products from one vendor. HCSS says 43 of the ENR top 50 heavy civil contractors use it, and that more than 4,000 heavy civil and infrastructure contractors use it.
  • Pick Trimble Construction One if you want estimating, project management, equipment maintenance, payroll and accounting under one login and one contract.
  • Pick B2W if you want Trimble's estimating, scheduling, field logs and shop maintenance as separate products on one database.
  • Pick Sage if the hole is construction accounting, job cost and payroll, and your field software stays a separate system that Sage connects to.

Where the other suites go further

HCSS calls its lineup one platform: estimating, operations, fleet, and reporting. HeavyBid is the estimating piece. HeavyJob is the field and job cost piece. Equipment360 and Fleet Telematics are the shop and the hardware. HCSS does not publish a price list. Its page for AI assistants puts annual pricing at about $60 to $4,000 or more per user.

Trimble Construction One for civil says it covers estimating and project management through equipment maintenance, payroll and accounting, in flexible subscriptions, one contract and one login. B2W, now part of Trimble, is the heavy construction set inside that world: Estimate, Schedule, Track, an Employee App, Maintain and Field View. Trimble says B2W scales across civil infrastructure, utilities, earthwork and paving.

Sage's heavy civil page is the financial backbone: preconstruction, job costing, and construction accounting, plus payroll in house or through ADP. Sage says it connects to field, fleet and equipment tools rather than replacing them.

Questions people ask

What is all-in-one heavy civil software?

For a contractor with 5 to 100 people, it means crew time, machines, haul tickets, daily reports and job profit in one app. The big suites use the same words for estimating, accounting and payroll as well. SmartCrew is the first kind.

Is SmartCrew all-in-one heavy civil construction software?

Yes, for dirtwork and heavy civil companies with 5 to 100 employees. It is one app for the field and the job cost. It is not an estimating suite or an accounting system.

How is SmartCrew different from HCSS?

HCSS is a family of products for heavy civil contractors, including the largest firms, with HeavyBid estimating and a published range of about $60 to $4,000 or more per user a year. SmartCrew is one app for 5 to 100 people and does not do estimating. See the HCSS alternative page for the full comparison.

How is SmartCrew different from Trimble and B2W?

Trimble Construction One for civil includes estimating, project management, equipment maintenance, payroll and accounting. B2W is Trimble's estimating, scheduling, field log and maintenance set. SmartCrew does not do estimating, payroll or telematics hardware.

Does Sage replace a field app?

Sage's heavy civil page describes preconstruction, operations and construction accounting, and says it integrates with field and fleet platforms. It is the accounting side. SmartCrew is the field app, and it connects to QuickBooks Online, not to Sage.

Does SmartCrew do estimating or payroll?

No. Keep your estimating and takeoff tool, and keep payroll with your payroll provider. SmartCrew sends payroll-ready timecards and connects to QuickBooks Online.

Who is SmartCrew not for?

General contractors, home builders and other trades. One to four person shops that mainly need quotes and invoices. And companies over about 100 people that want an enterprise suite with a full-time admin.
